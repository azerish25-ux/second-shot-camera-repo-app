# Phase 15 — prove useful retakes or make a bounded pivot

**Status: PLANNED / DEVICE EVIDENCE REQUIRED. No success is implied by this specification.**

**Dependencies:** the early phase-07 hardware gate and phases 08–14. Independent visual-design or documentation work may continue while this gate is blocked; monetization/release claims cannot bypass it. Read [acceptance gates](../ACCEPTANCE_GATES.md) and [the experiment protocol from phase 01](01-product-validation.md).

## Decision to make

Does guided handheld repetition produce a noticeably better, usable creative result than asking the same person to repeat the shot unaided? Compiling successfully, drawing an attractive path, or reporting small ARKit-derived errors does not answer that question. This gate determines whether to continue the restricted repeat-shot MVP, pivot to a framing/rehearsal assistant, or stop the unsupported capability.

## Preregister the experiment

Freeze the candidate commit, supported hardware/format matrix, cue policy, alignment limits, and success definitions before collecting the evaluation set. Keep tuning data separate. A proposed minimum pilot is 10 consenting creators, three short scenes, and guided/unguided conditions: **60 predeclared evaluation cases**, 30 per condition. Each case permits at most two paired-shot attempts; actual attempts can therefore exceed 60. Counterbalance order to reduce practice effects. Include at least three hardware classes: an older supported non-LiDAR iPhone, a newer non-LiDAR iPhone, and a supported Pro model. Report actual models and OS versions, not marketing classes alone.

Use a textured near-planar scene, a scene with foreground/background depth separation, and a safely accessible mixed-texture scene. Limit moves to 3–8 seconds, within a cleared area, without walking backwards or near stairs/roads. Use changing objects or performances only with participants' consent. Register every failed start and retry. Report first-attempt success and two-attempt-budget case success separately; missing output is a failure, not an exclusion. The usefulness thresholds below use the case denominator, not a cherry-picked set of completed clips.

## Measurements and implementation work

1. Add a versioned results schema and redacted report template under `docs/testing/`; raw media and room maps stay in the consented restricted evidence location. Record run ID, condition, participant code, device, commit, attempt count, elapsed setup time, interruptions, output availability, and reasons for refusal.
2. Measure camera-path error only when an independent reference setup supports it. Comparing two values generated by the same tracking system is not an absolute-accuracy measurement. For planar test scenes, holdout image landmarks can measure reprojection error; report depth-rich scenes separately.
3. Have two reviewers, blind to guided/unguided condition when feasible, judge whether the result is usable for the specified match cut/comparison. Define the rubric in advance: framing continuity, visible jumps, distortion, crop loss, and creator intent. Resolve disagreements transparently rather than selecting the favorable reviewer.
4. Collect completion time, subjective cue clarity, failed relocalizations, raw-output usefulness, and willingness to use the product again. Interest or a stated willingness to pay is not revenue validation.
5. Reopen every export and check source retention. Include interrupted/cancelled attempts, storage pressure, and at least one sustained-session thermal observation; these are separate reliability checks, not substitutions for the creative task.

## Provisional decision criteria

These thresholds are proposals to freeze before testing, not achieved results:

- [ ] At least 70% of the 30 guided cases produce a usable result within their two-attempt budget, improving the equivalent unaided-case rate by at least 20 percentage points. Also disclose first-attempt rates and total attempts for both conditions.
- [ ] At least 8 of 10 participants complete their first guided reference-to-reviewed-output flow within 10 minutes, with no more than two attempts per scene and no live developer coaching. Report later practice effects separately.
- [ ] No known origin-mixing, deceptive valid-tracking indication, source-data loss, unsafe cue, or silent failed export remains unresolved.
- [ ] Tracking failures and alignment refusals are intelligible to participants, with originals preserved when recoverable. Report all percentages with counts; a small pilot cannot establish a population-wide success guarantee.

## Continue, pivot, or stop

**Continue restricted MVP:** evidence meets the predeclared criteria; preserve device/scene limitations in the product copy. **Pivot:** capture is reliable but repetition benefit is weak; narrow to reference framing and rehearsal, revise the roadmap, and retest the new claim. **Stop dependent release work:** integrity/safety failures or no useful benefit. Do not silently lower thresholds after observing results; a changed hypothesis requires a new recorded experiment.

## Evidence and handoff

Publish only a redacted report with the frozen protocol, exact build, denominators, distributions, negative examples, reviewer disagreements, confidence limitations, and signed decision rationale from the owner/reviewer. `docs/STATUS.md` must say `BLOCKED_DEVICE` until this evidence exists. Commit the report and any approved scope change; never manufacture device evidence from simulator traces or screenshots.
